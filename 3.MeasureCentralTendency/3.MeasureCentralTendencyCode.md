

Created a list of numeric values for each category (day-wise bills).

# 🔷 **PART 2: Iris Dataset Statistics**

---

## 🔹 **Cell 6: Load Iris Dataset**

```python id="p2c1"
iris = pd.read_csv("iris.csv")

iris.head()
```

**Explanation:**
Loaded Iris dataset containing flower measurements.

---

## 🔹 **Cell 7: Filter by Species**

```python id="p2c2"
setosa = iris[iris['species'] == 'setosa']
versicolor = iris[iris['species'] == 'versicolor']
virginica = iris[iris['species'] == 'virginica']
```

**Explanation:**
Separated dataset based on species.

---

## 🔹 **Cell 8: Statistical Details**

```python id="p2c3"
print("Setosa Stats:\n", setosa.describe())
print("\nVersicolor Stats:\n", versicolor.describe())
print("\nVirginica Stats:\n", virginica.describe())
```

**Explanation:**
Displayed mean, std, min, max, and percentiles for each species.

---

## 🔹 **Cell 9: Percentiles Example**

```python id="p2c4"
print("Setosa Percentiles:\n", setosa.select_dtypes(include=np.number).quantile([0.25, 0.5, 0.75]))
```

**Explanation:**
Displayed quartiles (25%, 50%, 75%) for deeper analysis.

# 📘 **PS3 Viva Questions & Answers**

---

## 🔹 **Basic Concepts**

### 1. What is Descriptive Statistics?

**Answer:**
It summarizes and describes the main features of a dataset using measures like mean, median, and standard deviation.

---

### 2. What are measures of central tendency?

**Answer:**
They represent the central value of data:

* Mean
* Median
* Mode

---

### 3. What are measures of variability?

**Answer:**
They show the spread of data:

* Range
* Variance
* Standard deviation

---

## 🔹 **Mean, Median, Mode**

### 4. What is mean?

**Answer:**
Average of all values.

---

### 5. What is median?

**Answer:**
Middle value when data is sorted.

---

### 6. What is mode?

**Answer:**
Most frequently occurring value.

---

### 7. Which is affected by outliers?

**Answer:**
Mean is affected; median is not.

---

## 🔹 **Standard Deviation & Variance**

### 8. What is standard deviation?

**Answer:**
Measure of how spread out values are from the mean.

---

### 9. What is variance?

**Answer:**
Square of standard deviation.

---

### 10. What does high standard deviation mean?

**Answer:**
Data is widely spread.

---

### 11. What does low standard deviation mean?

**Answer:**
Data is closely clustered.

---

## 🔹 **Grouping**

### 12. Why did you group data by a categorical variable?

**Answer:**
To compare statistics across different categories.

---

### 13. What is groupby()?

**Answer:**
A pandas function used to split data into groups and apply operations.

---

### 14. What was your categorical variable?

**Answer:**
Example: `day` (in tips dataset) or `species` (in iris dataset).

---

### 15. What was your numeric variable?

**Answer:**
Example: `total_bill` or flower measurements.

---

## 🔹 **Percentiles**

### 16. What is a percentile?

**Answer:**
Value below which a percentage of observations fall.

---

### 17. What is 50th percentile?

**Answer:**
Median.

### 18. What are quartiles?

**Answer:**

* Q1 → 25%
* Q2 → 50%
* Q3 → 75%
